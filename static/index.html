YOUR IPHONE

Connect to your Mac

Open the same Workdesk on your iPhone. Tasks, notes and settings stay saved on your Mac. Keep the Mac awake, connected and running Workdesk.

  1. Install Tailscale on your Mac and iPhone. Sign in to the same Tailscale account and connect both devices.
  2. Enable MagicDNS and HTTPS Certificates in Tailscale DNS settings.
  3. Select Enable iPhone access below.

Tailscale provides the private connection; it does not host your Workdesk database. Its free Personal plan is for non-commercial personal use. Check plan eligibility.

Checking setup…

Open this address in Safari on your iPhone. Choose Share → Add to Home Screen, then open the Workdesk icon.

Your inbox updates while Workdesk is open on your phone. This connection does not enable iPhone alerts while the app is closed. Mac alerts keep their current settings.

CONTRIBUTION DISCOVERY

Find your shared work

Workdesk finds your PRs, reviews, and indexed commits, then scans open PRs in discovered repositories. Add other repositories to find commits on branches you did not open.

Co-author credits match your GitHub-linked, noreply, or configured email addresses. Only add addresses you use for your own commits. The history window follows Sync preferences.

STAY UP TO DATE

Notifications

Check for activity involving you while Workdesk is running. The first check quietly imports your existing unread inbox; new activity then triggers alerts.

GitHub may require a longer interval. On macOS, allow notifications from Script Editor / osascript in System Settings. Background checks use your gh auth login credentials.

LOCAL DATA

Saved on this computer

Tasks, GitHub snapshots, notes, and historical events are stored in SQLite. Export anytime; no application data is sent to a Workdesk server.

Automatic backups run once a day while Workdesk or its background checks run. The latest 7 automatic copies are kept; manual copies are kept until you delete them.

Backups include the whole Workdesk database, including notes and saved settings. Unfinished edits, GitHub credentials and macOS background schedules are not included.
